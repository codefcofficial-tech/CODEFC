How to make payment

  1. Copy the CODEFC account number displayed above.
  2. Open your Nigerian banking app and transfer the required amount.
  3. Before confirming, check that the account name displayed by your bank matches the CODEFC account name above.
  4. Use CODEFC VIP as your transfer narration if your banking app requests one.
  5. After a successful transfer, return here and enter the exact name registered to the bank account you used.
  6. Enter the actual transaction reference generated by your bank. Do not enter the narration as the reference.
  7. Submit your request and wait for Founder verification.

Never share your bank PIN, OTP, password or card details.
